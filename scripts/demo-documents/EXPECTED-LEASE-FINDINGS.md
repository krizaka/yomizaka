# Expected findings of the fictitious leases

The two lease fixtures of issue #302 are generated by `pnpm run demo:documents -- --out <dir>` (PDFs and `findings.json`, never committed). Everything in them is invented (see `docs/demo-data.md`).

| Fixture | Role | Generator id |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Clean filled lease | must raise **no FAIL** | `bail-rosemont-1-conforme` |
| Lease with known errors | the expected FAILs below | `bail-rosemont-5-clause-illegale` |
| Lease without the TAL notice | a missing mandatory notice (not part of #302's two fixtures; kept for the demo) | `bail-rosemont-4-sans-avis-tal` |

Every lease carries the mandatory mentions of the Québec form (parties, dwelling, term, rent, the lowest rent of the last twelve months, the tenant's right to remain). The clean lease adds the TAL notice; the errors are the only difference between the clean lease and the flawed one.

## Clean lease (`bail-rosemont-1-conforme`)

Expected: **no FAIL** on any rule. Every rule that passes carries a passage of the document. Parties (QC-LEASE-001, 002), rent (QC-LEASE-003), lowest rent (QC-LEASE-008, quote « Le loyer le plus bas payé au cours des douze derniers mois précédant le début du bail était de 1 200,00 $ par mois. ») pass with their passage; the advisory judgment rule (QC-LEASE-020) may be INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, never FAIL.

## Lease with known errors (`bail-rosemont-5-clause-illegale`)

Expected FAILs, each with its passage (quotes are the document's own words):

| Rule (pack `qc-residential-lease`) | Verdict | Article | Passage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| QC-LEASE-004 — deposit forbidden (only the first month in advance) | FAIL, BLOCKING | C.c.Q. 1904 al. 1 | « Le locataire verse un dépôt de sécurité de 950,00 $ à la signature. » |
| QC-LEASE-005 — post-dated cheques forbidden | FAIL, BLOCKING | C.c.Q. 1904 al. 2 | « Le locataire remet douze chèques postdatés. » |
| QC-LEASE-006 — refusing children is without effect | FAIL, BLOCKING | C.c.Q. 1899 | « Il est interdit d'avoir des enfants dans le logement. » |
| QC-LEASE-020 — abusive clause (judgment) | FAIL, ADVISORY | C.c.Q. 1901 | « Il est interdit d'avoir des enfants dans le logement. » |

Expected **not** to fail: QC-LEASE-007 (no waiver of the right to remain: the clause is absent here), QC-LEASE-008 (the lowest-rent notice is present), the TAL notice (present).

The rule numbers come from the recorded defective run of the same ruleset (`apps/web/content/pack-proofs/qc-residential-lease--fr-defective.json`, pack 2024-02-21). The rule IDs of the engine's current pack must be confirmed on the new recording before the test asserts them.

## Lease without the TAL notice (`bail-rosemont-4-sans-avis-tal`)

Expected: a FAIL on the rule for the « Renseignements au nouveau locataire d'un logement » notice (BLOCKING, the generator's code `QC-LEASE-TAL-NOTICE`). The recorded pack of 2024-02-21 has no rule of that name among its ten, so the engine's rule for it is to be confirmed. Its absence is quoted nowhere: a FAIL here has no passage, and the test must not accept a PASS in its place.

## Rule of the test

- Each expected FAIL is returned with the article and a passage that is in the document text.
- No PASS is accepted without a passage (`evidenceSpan` non-null, quote contained in the text); the pre-fix recording shows PASS with no passage (`QC-LEASE-004` to `007`, `009`), which orazaka-packs#127 (merged) forbids.
- The clean lease raises no FAIL.
