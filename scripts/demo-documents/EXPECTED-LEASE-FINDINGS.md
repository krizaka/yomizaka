# Expected findings of the fictitious leases

The lease fixtures of issue #302 are generated by `pnpm run demo:documents -- --out <dir>` (PDFs and `findings.json`, never committed). Everything in them is invented (see `docs/demo-data.md`). The engine's answers to the two fixtures are recorded in `apps/web/content/demo-lease-runs/` (`scripts/record-demo-leases.mjs`) and asserted by `apps/web/lib/demo-lease-runs.test.ts`.

| Fixture | Role | Generator id |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Clean filled lease | must raise **no FAIL** | `bail-rosemont-1-conforme` |
| Lease with known errors | the expected FAILs below | `bail-rosemont-5-clause-illegale` |
| Lease without the TAL notice | a missing mandatory notice (kept for the demo, not recorded) | `bail-rosemont-4-sans-avis-tal` |

Every lease names the lessor and the lessee with a « domicilié » mention (the pack's section A reads it), and gives the lowest rent of the last twelve months (section G). The errors are the only difference between the clean lease and the flawed one.

## Clean lease (`bail-rosemont-1-conforme`), engine blueprint 1.4.0

Recorded verdicts: 6 PASS, 6 NOT_APPLICABLE, **0 FAIL**. The PASS verdicts quote the passage they judged: QC-LEASE-001 (« Locateur : … domicilié »), 002 (« Locataire : … domicilié »), 007 (« maintien dans les lieux »), 008 (« loyer le plus bas »), 009 (« hausse »); QC-LEASE-003 quotes the rent. The deposit, children and post-dated cheque rules are NOT_APPLICABLE: the lease does not address them.

## Lease with known errors (`bail-rosemont-5-clause-illegale`), engine blueprint 1.4.0

Expected and recorded FAILs, each with its passage (the document's own words) and the article the pack cites:

| Rule (pack `qc-residential-lease`) | Verdict | Article | Passage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| QC-LEASE-004 — a deposit is forbidden beyond the first month in advance | FAIL, BLOCKING | C.c.Q. 1904 | « dépôt de sécurité de 950,00 $ » |
| QC-LEASE-005 — post-dated cheques are forbidden | FAIL, BLOCKING | C.c.Q. 1904 | « chèques postdatés » |
| QC-LEASE-006 — refusing children is without effect | FAIL, BLOCKING | C.c.Q. 1899 | « interdit d'avoir des enfants » |
| QC-LEASE-020 — abusive clause (judgment) | FAIL, ADVISORY | C.c.Q. 1901 | « Il est interdit d'avoir des enfants dans le logement. » |

The other rules PASS with a passage (001, 002, 003, 007, 008, 009) or are NOT_APPLICABLE.

## Lease without the TAL notice (`bail-rosemont-4-sans-avis-tal`)

Not recorded yet. The pack's ten rules of the 2024-02-21 ruleset have no rule for the « Renseignements au nouveau locataire d'un logement » notice, so the engine is not expected to fail it. Recording it would confirm this.

## What the test asserts

- Each recording is the text the generator writes today (no drift).
- A PASS always carries the passage it judged, and every quote is a passage of the document (no hollow PASS).
- The clean lease raises no FAIL.
- The errors lease raises exactly the four FAILs above, each with its quote and its article.
